**Paper accepted at NeurIPS 2026**:

* The paper [Permutation-Invariant Spectral Learning via Dyson Diffusion](https://openreview.net/group?id=NeurIPS.cc/2026/Conference/Authors&referrer=%5BHomepage%5D(%2F)),
  with Tassilo Schwarz, Cai Dieball, Constantin Kogler, Renaud Lambiotte,
  Arnaud Doucet and Aljaz Godec was accepted as a Poster at NeurIPS 2026.
  In it we design a diffusion model focused on Dyson's Brownian motion to learn
  spectra without the need for permutation-equivariant learning architectures,
  while preserving all remaining information.
